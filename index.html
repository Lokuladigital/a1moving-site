About us

Local crew. Careful hands.

A1Moving was founded right here in Edmonton by Allan Laku with one simple idea — treat every move like it's your own. It's locally owned and owner-operated: no franchise layers, no call centres, and Allan is directly involved in how every crew is trained and every move is run.

  • The same crew wraps, loads, drives and unloads
  • Pads, shrink wrap and straps on every job
  • A direct line to the owner, not a call centre
